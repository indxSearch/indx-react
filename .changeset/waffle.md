`Waffle`: shares of a whole as a grid of pixel squares, each segment its share of the cells filled column by column from the left, the first in the ink and the rest down the tone ladder, with a legend beneath. `columns`, `rows` and `cell` set the shape (20 by 5 at 4px by default, so a cell is a percent); the ladder has four steps, so a fifth segment, or one that means something, takes a `color`. Where a pie would be, this is the pixel version.
