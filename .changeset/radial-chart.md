`RadialChart`: one series around a clock face, a bar a sample from the top clockwise with its length the value, around a hollow centre that can hold a Stat; a thin guide a sample to the rim and a dashed line at the start. Hover reads a bar by its angle, with `format` for the tooltip. A year of days, the hours of a day.
