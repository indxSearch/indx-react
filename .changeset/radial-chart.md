`RadialChart`: bars around a clock face from the top clockwise, around a hollow centre that can hold a Stat. One series, a bar a sample with its length the value, a thin guide a sample to the rim and a dashed line at the start; several `series` sharing each sample's slot, each in its colour, with a legend; or `groups`, the rose, a sector of bars per group at the group's value over a dim track with the name and value in it. Hover reads a bar by its angle, with `format` for the tooltip. The hours of a day, searches and asks by hour, a handful of kinds compared.
