On a catalogue, the assistant's picks are the result list while the conversation is open, with nothing for the page to wire. The answer chooses the records that answer the visitor by reading them, best first, each with why. `AskPanel` puts them on the `SearchProvider` (`picks`, `showPicks`); `SearchResults` lists them in place of the search, numbered as the answer cites them, with the reason under each row, and a way back ("Show the search instead", or "Show all 45 found" when the picks came with a search); `SearchInput` drops its Nearest and No results chips, which are about the search. Typing, Clear, a new conversation or the panel going gives the list back. A proactive answer leaves the list alone. `AskPanel`'s `onPicks` reports them and `listPicks={false}` keeps them in the panel; `SearchResults` takes `picks` and `onShowSearch` to override. A pick opened is reported against the answer, and a follow-up sends the picks as what the last turn showed (`AskTurn.shown.picks`). New types `AskPick`, `AskPicks`.
