TreeCanvas is a board: drag it or scroll with two fingers to pan, pinch or Ctrl/Cmd and the wheel to zoom at the pointer, with zoom in, zoom out, fit and the zoom shown in its corner, and the arrows, + - and 0 from the keyboard. It opens fitted to the tree. Handles mark where the edges leave and arrive, and a dashed edge leads to a node kept only as the way down to a match. `pattern` lays a systm pattern behind it, such as `'grid-12'`, which follows the pan and the zoom. `pan={false}` makes it a still picture. `zoom` is now the zoom it opens at; left out, it fits.
