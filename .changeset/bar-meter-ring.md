`BarMeter` `shape="ring"`: the same bars around a circle, from the top clockwise, with the value in the middle and the label beneath. `size` sets the diameter, 72px by default; 48 is the smallest that holds a short value and fits a Stat as its value, and from 96 the ceiling joins the value on a second line. `label` is now optional in both shapes.
