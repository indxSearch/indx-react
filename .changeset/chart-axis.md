`Chart`: `yAxis` ("left" or "right") puts round ticks on the value axis, each with a faint gridline, and widens the range to them; `yFormat` sets their text, compact by default (1.2k, 3M). `references` are levels to read the data against, a threshold, an average, a quota: each a dashed line with its label in a tag at the right, in the ink or in a colour such as the signal red for a limit. A reference outside the data pulls the range out to include it.
