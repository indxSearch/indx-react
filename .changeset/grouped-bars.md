`GroupedBars`: thin bars in labelled groups across the width, a group a day and a bar an hour, or a group a week and a bar a day. A hairline marks where each group starts with its name and a figure above; a band under each group takes its colour when it is highlighted, so one group stands out; the sample labels sit under the bands, as many as fit; hover reads a bar.
