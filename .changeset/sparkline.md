New `Sparkline`: a tiny display-only graph for putting a trend next to a number — searches over the last hours, documents added per day. Line or bar, no axis, legend, tooltip or interaction. It takes its colour from whatever it sits in and fills the width it is given, so it drops into a card or a table cell without configuration. `Chart` is unchanged and remains the one to use when you want any of the chrome.
