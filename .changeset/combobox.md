Combobox: a Select you can type into, for a list too long to scroll. Closed it is a Select; open, a search box sits over the options. Typing narrows them, labels that start with what was typed first; the arrows move the highlight, Enter chooses and Escape closes, and the field gets its focus back. Pass `search` for a search of your own.
