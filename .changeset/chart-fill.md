`Chart`: `fill` per series. `solid` is the series colour, flat on a bar and faint under a line, so a single series reads as an area; `hatch`, `dots` and `checker` are drawn in the colour on the 2px pixel grid, so two or three series tell apart in one tone. A bar is solid unless told otherwise; a line stays a wire unless given an area. The legend shows a patterned series' pattern.
