`BarSlider`: a slider that is its own row. The fill runs from the left edge to the value and spans the full height, the label sits inside at the left, the value at the right, and the handle is a thin line at the fill's edge. Sized like a Button (`default` 40px, `micro` 30px), so a stack of settings lines up with fields. `stepMarks` draws a dot per step on the track, `format` shapes the value shown.
