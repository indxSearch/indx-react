`SegmentedControl`: one choice from a few, side by side in a hairline frame with one cell active, for the period of a chart, the interval of a graph, the type of a view. A cell is a word, an icon, or both; `size` micro (22px) or default (28px); `stretch` shares the width; arrow keys move between cells.
