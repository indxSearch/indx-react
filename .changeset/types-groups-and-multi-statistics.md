Types for one search per keystroke: `GroupedSearchRequest`, `SearchGroup` and `GroupedSearchResult` for `search/groups` (one text answered in named sections, each with its own filter, rows and count), `MultiSearchQuery` for a `search/multi` search that says for itself whether it counts in the statistics, and `includeFields` on `QueryProxy` to get only the record fields a page shows.
