`Waffle`: hovering a cell names its segment in a tooltip beside it, with its share, and a cell or a legend entry under the pointer dims the other segments, so which is which can be browsed from either side. A segment also takes an `icon`, a small graphic before its label in the legend and the tooltip, about the text's height: a pixl icon at 14, or a flag.
