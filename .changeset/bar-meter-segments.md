`BarMeter` `segments`: the row as shares of a whole instead of a level, each segment its share of the bars in a tone of its own, its name and share above it and a hairline between. Holders by kind, documents by type. A segment under 8 % of the row gets no head, the last one apart, and `labels="legend"` lists all segments under the bars instead. `value` and `max` are optional, since the segments are the whole.
