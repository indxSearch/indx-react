`Heatmap`: a grid of pixel cells toned by value, columns of `rows` cells filled top to bottom then the next column, zero the empty tone and the rest four steps up to the ink, with column labels beneath and row labels at the left. `calendarCells(from, to, valueOf)` lays days out as weeks across and weekdays down, Monday first, with month labels. For days a dataset woke, searches by hour and weekday, any count per cell of a regular grid.
