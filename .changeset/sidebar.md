Sidebar: the side column of an app, groups of Buttons with the open one primary. Cmd or Ctrl and B, or the button at its foot, folds it to a rail of icons, where each item names itself in a Tooltip and a badge is a dot; `storageKey` keeps the viewer's choice. A group with `collapsible` opens and closes under its title. At 768px and narrower it is a drawer over the page, opened by the host (`open`, `onOpenChange`) and closed by Escape, a press beside it or a choice.
