`BarMeter`: a level against its ceiling as a row of bars, the filled ones in the ink and the rest dim, with a label above and the value and ceiling below. For CPU pressure, memory in use, a quota: where ProgressBar is a percentage on its way to done, this goes up and down. `color` paints the filled bars, for a level past a threshold.
