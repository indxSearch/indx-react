The hourglass spinner turns clockwise. It had four frames: three upright with the sand at the bottom, one sideways with the sand on the left, so each loop made a clockwise quarter turn and then an anticlockwise one back to upright, and the return read as the rotation. Three upside-down frames, derived by flipping the upright ones, now sit between: sand at the top trickling, sand at the bottom, sideways, back to the top. Every turn goes the same way, and the loop is the real hourglass cycle. No new artwork; the frames are the same pixels flipped.
