`Stat`: three rows, the label at the top, the value in the middle and the foot at the bottom. The middle takes the height the other two leave and centres what it holds, so in a `StatGroup` the labels line up at the top, the feet at the bottom, and a ring, a number and a chip sit on one middle line. A foot without a figure now spans the tile's width.
